# Neo voice

Requires the BlackStory checkout. See [loading and capabilities](../../../docs/research/README.md#skills-and-harness-portability)
for tool requirements and use from any harness.

Read [the voice contract](../../../docs/content/neo-voice.md). Its purpose is
specific, readable history whose scope survives checking. No persona, cadence or
narrative arc overrides evidence.

1. Identify the reader's question and surface. A record summary needs identity,
   significance and the relevant place/time; it does not need a miniature chapter.
2. Use the reviewed fact base and [fact protocol](../../../docs/methodology/chapter-fact-validation.md).
   Map factual clauses to evidence. Missing support is a research need or a reason
   to omit the clause, never an invitation to complete it from model memory.
3. Choose the structure the material supports: direct answer, chronology, documented
   scene or comparison. Do not manufacture an opening person, imagined experience,
   obstacle/pathway pair, present-day lesson or agency ending.
4. Draft with concrete nouns, precise verbs and varied natural sentences. Preserve
   decisive qualifiers and uncertainty in the visible text. Mark interpretations
   and attribute testimony. Avoid generic praise, repeated moral summaries and
   claims of significance that the facts do not establish.
5. Read aloud, then compare with neighboring records. Remove repetitive templates,
   redundant conclusions and decorative detail. No fixed rhetorical quotas or
   sentence-length pattern. Short, accurate prose is preferable to padding.
6. Apply [prose review](../blackstory-prose-review/SKILL.md), then the proportionate
   [adversarial review](../blackstory-ringer-review/SKILL.md). A factual change returns to
   evidence review. Run the actual surface's gates, not article commands for an entity.

Preserve verbatim quotations, including their grammar; identify the speaker. Do not
invent interiority, dialogue or weather. No em dashes in authored public prose.
Use contractions naturally under the current mechanical style checks. Those checks
detect a few forms; they do not detect truth, dignity or good writing.

Deliver the draft, its evidence mapping and any unresolved review findings. Do not
equate fluent prose with publication readiness.

## Working contract

**Input:** Reviewed assertions, their qualifiers and the reader surface.

**Output:** Natural draft prose with a sentence-to-evidence map.

**Boundary and handoff:** Hand copy to prose and ringer review. New factual detail reopens claim review; no mandatory padding or narrative template.

**Capabilities:** Follow [capability and permission limits](../../../docs/research/README.md#capabilities-and-permissions).
Use the active harness’s available tools and report unsupported steps. Operational flags stay
in the linked operations contract; no proprietary client or model family is required.
