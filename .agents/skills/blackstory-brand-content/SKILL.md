# Brand content

Copy outside the archive itself: pages that explain the project, and posts that send readers
to a record. Binding sources, read before writing: [brand story and voice](../../../docs/ui/story.md)
(thesis, register, the speaker rule, never-list), [brand usage](../../../docs/ui/brand.md)
(mark, imagery, map dignity) and [Neo's contract](../../../docs/content/neo-voice.md) for register.
This skill adds only what those do not say.

## Decision order

1. **Name the surface and the speaker.** Site and marketing copy has two speakers: the archive
   (what the software does) and Gerald as "I" (a choice or commitment). Never "we". Social posts
   are the archive speaking; no first person at all.
2. **Start from something published.** Every factual clause in a post or a marketing page comes
   from a published record, chapter or the methodology page, and carries the same qualifiers.
   Copy the record's scope; do not sharpen it. If the fact is not published, it is a research
   request, not copy. Unpublished drafts, pending candidates and model memory are never sources.
3. **Lead with presence.** The place, person or institution and what is documented about it.
   Violence and loss are stated plainly when they are the record, never used as the hook.
4. **One link, to the record.** A post points at the record or chapter that supports it, so the
   evidence is one tap away. Do not cite the underlying source in place of the record.
5. **Dates.** An on-this-day post needs a claim with day precision on that record. A year or
   month is not a day; skip the post.
6. **Images.** Only the record's own published image, uncropped of its credit, with the credit
   and date basis in the post or alt text. Nothing generated that depicts a person, place,
   building, document, map or event. No image is better than a stock one. Always write alt text.
7. **Living people.** No post about a living or unknown-status person's residence, family or
   whereabouts. When unsure, treat as living and skip.
8. **Check claims about the project itself.** Counts, coverage and review claims ("every claim
   is checked by...") must match what is true today; query the number, date it, and never round
   up. No completeness claims. The site states a reviewing agent on a different model checks
   each claim and names no vendor; say nothing stronger.
9. **Review before it leaves.** Run [prose review](../blackstory-prose-review/SKILL.md); for
   anything with a factual clause also the compact pass of
   [ringer review](../blackstory-ringer-review/SKILL.md).

## Posts

- Plain sentences, one idea, the place and year early. No hashtag stacks (two at most, real
  ones), no emoji as decoration, no threads that restate the record.
- Per-platform length is a limit, not a target. Write the post once and trim.
- Never: "discover", "unlock", "did you know", "hidden history", "untold", rhetorical-question
  hooks, exclamation points, calls to share, engagement bait, or commentary on current events
  and living public figures.
- A batch should not read as a template. Vary the opening and the shape across a week.

## Delivery

Posts are **drafts only**. Save them as drafts in the scheduling tool or hand them over as a
list with record link, image, credit and alt text. Never publish or schedule a post, reply to
anyone, or name an official account that has not been reserved
([official properties](../../../docs/ui/official-properties.md)). Site copy lands as a normal
code change on `staging`.

**Never:** invent a fact, quote or number for reach; restate a record more confidently than
it reads; use a person as anonymous decoration; expose pipeline words (quarantine, graylist,
research case, landscape) in public copy.
