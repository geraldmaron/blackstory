# Engineering decisions

[Architecture](https://github.com/geraldmaron/blackstory/blob/main/docs/architecture.md) identifies
current implementation owners and the challenge procedure. The
[engineering contract](https://github.com/geraldmaron/blackstory/blob/main/docs/decisions-carryover.md)
and [research framework](https://github.com/geraldmaron/blackstory/blob/main/docs/research/README.md)
own their respective constraints and known gaps.

Challenge a rule against observed behavior, its strongest failure mode, and the best viable
alternative. Update the owning contract and its enforcement together. Historical ADRs and PRDs
are evidence of prior reasoning, not competing authority or a reason to add another decision memo.
