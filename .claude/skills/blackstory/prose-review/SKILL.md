# Prose review

Judgment playbook. The binding standard is
[`docs/content/neo-voice.md`](../../../../docs/content/neo-voice.md); this skill walks
it sentence by sentence. Prose review assumes the evidence work is done (facts
validated, citations attached); it does not re-litigate sources. That is
[`blackstory-claim-corroborate`](../claim-corroborate/SKILL.md) upstream and
[`blackstory-ringer-review`](../ringer-review/SKILL.md) downstream. This pass answers
one question: is the writing the writing we publish?

## The pass, in order

Walk the full draft once per lens. Quote every finding; never paraphrase a problem.

1. **Structural laws** (`neo-voice.md` Part II). Opening is a person in a place at a
   time. Every documented obstacle has a cited pathway somewhere. The final movement
   lands on cited agency without softening what came before. Any miss here is a
   blocker, not a note.
2. **Register** (Part I). Hunt performed outrage, surprise, strain, adjectives ahead
   of their evidence, paragraphs explaining prior paragraphs, sermon paragraphs,
   throat-clearing. The narrator is felt in selection and cadence only.
3. **Claim grammar** (Part V). Flag every interpretive claim written as flat fact,
   every smuggled moral claim, every causal verb outside a gated causal citation,
   every dispute resolved in prose that the sources leave open.
4. **Sentence mechanics** (Part IV). One subordinate clause per sentence. Varied
   cadence. No staccato fragments. Count the budgets: anaphora runs ≤ 2, jeremiad ≤ 1
   and only in the closing third, one-sentence paragraphs ≤ 3, flourish ≤ 1,
   call-and-response ≤ 2. Verify the flourish draws only on facts already cited.
5. **Word level** (Part IV). Em dashes in narrative prose; expanded contractions;
   hype words; uplift clichés; stacked adjectives before a number; hedging
   boilerplate; bare decimals or lone percents in prose; the diction table
   (enslaved person / self-liberated / massacre / named people / agency verbs), with
   the record's vocabulary quoted as testimony, never adopted.
6. **Standalone + era mechanics** (Parts III, V). No site references, sibling-chapter
   references, or publisher first person. Tense and person hold inside each era;
   shifts only at jump-cuts. Method-note material is in method notes, not the
   narrative. Second person consistent inside eras.

## Output

A findings list: lens, quoted sentence, what it violates (cite the `neo-voice.md`
part), proposed rewrite. Then a verdict: **pass / fix-then-pass**. Findings are fixed,
not argued with; if the drafter believes a finding is wrong, that dispute goes to a
human, not into silent non-compliance.

## Do / Never

**Do:** quote every finding verbatim; cite the exact rule; propose the rewrite; check
the budgets by actually counting.

**Never:** soften a factual claim to fix a voice problem (voice findings are fixed
with craft, not with retreat); add new factual material during review (that reopens
research); pass a draft because it is close.
