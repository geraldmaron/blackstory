Fixed in every direction

  • Memorial is untouched (P-01). Lives, the Door and Explore are out of scope.
  • The four typefaces, Archive Paper, Black Ink and Copper stay.
  • No stock imagery, no sepia filters, no AI images as documentary. People are always identified.
  • Nothing implies a law is joined to a record (P-02).
  • One find bar: Enter submits, no Apply button, 44px targets on phones, works without JavaScript.
  • Every room gets a jump nav, plated chapters, honest stats and a closing handoff. That part is the same under A, B and C.

Placeholders in this mockup

  • Direction B shows typeset stand-ins where a scanned document would go. Each is tagged "Media slot" or "Example". No real scans are embedded.
  • The "How to read this catalog" facts on Law are new copy drafted from the live lede.
  • Stats on Methodology and Support are drawn from sentences already on those pages.
