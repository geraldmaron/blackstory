# Hosting or provider interruption

Identify the affected layer: domain/DNS, Vercel application hosting, Supabase database/auth/storage,
or a source institution. Preserve the provider notice, support case and impact scope.

1. Open the relevant provider's support or appeal channel. Record deadlines and required evidence.
2. Verify off-provider database and private-object recovery copies before account access is lost.
3. Keep the last verified public release available only where its rights and privacy controls
   remain enforceable. Do not redirect readers to an unverified or stale private-data export.
4. Use [backup and recovery](../backup-restore.md) to restore into an isolated destination and
   validate counts, hashes, authorization and public projections before switching traffic.
5. Document degraded behavior and restore normal writes only after the replacement passes checks.

No alternate cloud platform is automatically provisioned. A fallback that exists only in a design
document is not recovery capacity. Do not remove or bypass access restrictions to recover a source.
