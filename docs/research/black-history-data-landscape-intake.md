# Black history source portfolio

Use the [research method](README.md) and [operations](research-operations.md) as current authority.
This portfolio records acquisition choices to challenge with measured yield, source fitness,
rights, preservation coverage and cost. It does not certify a live corpus count or an open API.

## Priorities

- Place and institution registries can seed overlooked local records. NRHP Multiple Property
  Listings and nomination files often add context unavailable in a short registry row. Registry
  inclusion establishes a designation, not every historical claim about the subject.
- Black newspapers, HBCU collections, community archives and consented oral histories can reveal
  people, organizations and relationships underrepresented in national catalogs. Record editorial
  context, cataloging bias, digitization gaps and restrictions rather than ranking these sources
  below institutional catalogs by default.
- DPLA and other aggregators improve discovery. Trace records to their holding institution and
  original item; copies and syndication do not count as independent corroboration.
- Wikidata identifiers and qualified statements can suggest namesakes, aliases and neighboring
  entities. Preserve source predicates, qualifiers, references and intermediate nodes. Identity
  or a racial classification alone cannot establish a historical relationship.
- Enslaved.org and other domain collections need field, license, provenance and identity review
  before a bounded import. Dataset scale is not a quality metric.

## Admission and evaluation

Start with rights-cleared fixtures and a bounded sample. Compare retrieval by identifiers,
aliases, lexical/OCR variants, place/time context and vectors. Include undigitized or inaccessible
holdings in the gap record. Absence in a search result is not evidence that a person did not exist.

Measure unique evidence and supported relationships per unit of work, false merges, unsupported
edges and preservation failures. Keep a held-out sample of obscure entities and contradictions.
Evaluate another domain through the same profile and contracts before calling the harness reusable.
No bulk model/OCR budget or marketed coverage claim follows from a promising sample alone.

## Delivery boundary

Readers receive released projections and original/archived source links. Private canonical,
research and evidence records remain outside anonymous access. Research proposes; independent
review and publication gates decide release. Public rendering never calls an LLM.

For capacity and acquisition budgets use [Supabase cost controls](supabase-pro-cost-envelope.md).
Current execution status belongs in Beads; architectural changes follow the challenge procedure
in [Architecture](../architecture.md), without creating a competing ADR or PRD archive.
