# Lives Across the Decades — NHGIS published-table spec, 1870 / 1880 / 1890 / 1900

Every code below was returned by a live call to `https://api.ipums.org/metadata/nhgis/...?version=2`
on 2026-09-15. Universe strings and variable descriptions are quoted verbatim from the API. Nothing
here is inferred from printed volumes.

## Facts that hold for all four decades

- **No dataset in these decades has `breakdowns`.** The API returns `"breakdowns": null` for
  1870_cPAX, 1870_sPHX, 1870_sRN, 1870_sROG, 1880_cPAX, 1880_sPHX, 1880_sPbSMX, 1890_cPHAM,
  1890_sPHA, 1890_cAg, 1900_cPHAM, 1900_cAg and 1900_sOcc. An extract needs **no** `breakdownValues`
  for any of them; NHGIS applies its default total-area breakdown. This differs from the
  1980/1990/2000 pattern in `packages/ops-data/src/lives/decennial.ts`, where every dataset carries
  an explicit `breakdownValues` list.
- **Geography.** Every dataset used here has `nation` ("Nation") and `state` ("State"). The `_c*`
  datasets add `county` ("State--County"); the `_s*` datasets are nation+state only. Nothing below is
  county-only.
- **Hispanic origin is a gap in all four decades, in every table.** No variable in any 1870, 1880,
  1890 or 1900 NHGIS table carries a Spanish-origin, Hispanic, Mexican or surname concept. The race
  vocabularies are only: White, Colored, Negro, Mulatto/Quadroon/Octoroon (1890 only), Chinese,
  Japanese, Indian, "Other Colored". **Do not substitute a proxy and do not write a Hispanic figure
  for these decades.** Every Hispanic cell for 1870-1900 is `not_measured`.
- **Race label traps, decade by decade.**
  - 1870 and 1880 tables use **"Colored"** as a category alongside separate "Chinese" and "Indian"
    columns, so "Colored" in those tables is Black (Negro + Mulatto as enumerated).
  - 1890 and 1900 tables use **"Colored"** as an *aggregate of all non-white people* (Negro +
    Chinese + Japanese + Indian). Where a table also publishes **"Negro"**, that is the Black figure;
    where it publishes only "Colored", the figure is not Black alone.
  - 1890 alone splits Black people by blood fraction (Negroes / Mulattoes / Quadroons / Octoroons).

# 1870

Datasets used:
- **1870_cPAX** — "Population, Agriculture & Other Data [US, States & Counties]"
- **1870_sPHX** — "Population, Housing & Other Data [US & States]"
- **1870_sROG** — "Religious Bodies, Occupation & Government Data [US & States]"

## 1870 / population_share — VERIFIED

- Dataset: **1870_cPAX**, "Population, Agriculture & Other Data [US, States & Counties]"
- Table: **NT4**, nhgisCode prefix **AK3**, description "Race"
- Universe: `Persons`
- geogLevels confirmed: nation, state, county
- breakdownValues: none required (dataset has no breakdowns)
- Variables:
  - `AK3001` = "White"
  - `AK3002` = "Colored"  ← Black
  - `AK3003` = "Chinese"
  - `AK3004` = "Indian"
- Denominator (everyone): **1870_cPAX NT1**, prefix **AJ3**, "Total Population", universe `Persons`
  - `AJ3001` = "Total"
- Trap: 1870 is the census with the known Southern undercount (the Census Office itself doubted the
  1870 returns for several ex-Confederate states). "Colored" here excludes Chinese and Indian, so it
  is Black; that is *not* true of the 1890/1900 "Colored".

## 1870 / urban — NOT AVAILABLE (and not required; the urban condition starts 1890)

- Reason: neither is crossed by race.

## 1870 / homeownership — NOT AVAILABLE (and not required; the condition starts 1900)

- Reason: 1870 has no home-tenure table of any kind in NHGIS. Home ownership was first asked in 1890.

Denominator: **NOT AVAILABLE for persons 10 and over by race.** No 1870 NHGIS table publishes

- **1870_sPHX NT47 / AMT** "Male Population 21 Years of Age and Over by Race", universe
  `Males 21 Years and Over`: `AMT001` White, `AMT002` Colored, `AMT003` Chinese, `AMT004` Indian

numerator `AKA005` (White) and `AKA011` (Colored) from 1870_cPAX NT16; denominator `AMT001` and
`AMT002` from 1870_sPHX NT47. Nation and state on both.

Trap: this is a *males 21 and over* rate, not the 10-and-over rate the other decades give, and it is
a "cannot write" measure (1870_cPAX NT13 / AJ7 publishes "cannot read" but only as a single total,
`AJ7001` = "Total", universe `Persons 10 Years and Over Who Cannot Read`). Do not chart it on the same
line as an 1880 or 1900 figure without saying the universe changed.

  - `ASA011` = "Colored >> 21 years of age and over >> Male"
  - `ASA012` = "Colored >> 21 years of age and over >> Female"

Denominator:
- Dataset: **1880_sPHX** (same dataset, same extract)
- Table: **NT18**, prefix **AR9**, "Persons 10 Years of Age and Over by Race by Age by Sex"
- Universe: `Persons 10 Years and Over`
- Variables: `AR9001`-`AR9006` White and `AR9007`-`AR9012` Colored, in the identical

Trap: the race-crossed measure is **cannot write**; the **cannot read** figure (AR6) is published
only as an all-races total. Do not mix them. Nativity detail for white people is in NT16 / AR7 and
NT17 / AR8, which have no Colored counterpart.

## 1890 / population_share — VERIFIED

Black:
- Dataset: **1890_cPHAM**, "Population, Housing, Agriculture & Manufacturing Data [US, States & Counties]"
- Table: **NT4**, prefix **AVF**, "Non-White Population by Race by Year"
- Universe: `Non-White Persons`
- geogLevels confirmed: nation, state, county
- Variables (the 1890 column is the one to read):
  - `AVF001` = "Negro >> 1890"  ← Black
  - `AVF002` = "Negro >> 1880", `AVF003` = "Negro >> 1870"
  - `AVF004` = "Chinese >> 1890", `AVF005` = "Chinese >> 1880", `AVF006` = "Chinese >> 1870"
  - `AVF007` = "Japanese >> 1890", `AVF008` = "Japanese >> 1880", `AVF009` = "Japanese >> 1870"
  - `AVF010` = "Civilized Indian >> 1890", `AVF011` = "... >> 1880", `AVF012` = "... >> 1870"

White:
- **1890_cPHAM NT61**, prefix **AV2**, "Total White Population", universe `White Persons`,
  `AV2001` = "Total". (By sex: **NT60 / AV1**, `AV1001` Male, `AV1002` Female.)

Everyone:
- **1890_cPHAM NT1**, prefix **AUM**, "Total Population", universe `Persons`, `AUM001` = "Total".

Traps:
1. **1890_cPHAM NT6 / AV0** "Race/Nativity by Sex" (universe `Persons`) looks like the natural
   population table but its Black rows are labeled "Colored": `AV0001`-`AV0006` are
   "White: Native-born of native parentage / Native-born of foreign parentage / Foreign-born" x
   Male / Female, and `AV0007` = "Colored >> Male", `AV0008` = "Colored >> Female". In 1890 "Colored"
   is Negro + Chinese + Japanese + civilized Indian. **Do not read AV0007/AV0008 as Black.** Use
   `AVF001`.
2. **1890_sPHA NT3 / AWL** "Non-White Population by Race" (universe `Non-White Persons`, nation +
   state) splits Black people by blood fraction: `AWL001` "Negroes", `AWL002` "Mulattoes",
   `AWL003` "Quadroons", `AWL004` "Octoroons", `AWL005` "Chinese", `AWL006` "Japanese",
   `AWL007` "Civilized Indians". Here `AWL001` alone is **not** the Black total — the Black total is
   `AWL001 + AWL002 + AWL003 + AWL004`, which is what `AVF001` already gives. Prefer AVF001.

- **1890_cPHAM NT20 / AUV** "Number of Farms by Type of Farm Tenure by Acreage".
- Reason: no race crossing on any of them.

## 1890 / work-based class (occupation by race) — NOT AVAILABLE

- **Reason: NHGIS publishes no occupation table for 1890 at all**, race-crossed or otherwise. 1870
  (1870_sROG) and 1900 (1900_sOcc) each have an occupation dataset; 1880 has the block inside
  1880_sPHX; 1890 has none — the three 1890 datasets are population/housing/agriculture,
  agriculture, and religious bodies.
- The class tier for the 1890s therefore cannot be built from NHGIS published tables in any form.

# 1900

Datasets used:
- **1900_cPHAM** — "Population, Housing, Agriculture & Manufacturing Data [US, States & Counties]"
- **1900_cAg** — "Agriculture Data [US, States & Counties]"
- **1900_sOcc** — "Occupation Data [US & States]"

## 1900 / population_share — VERIFIED

Black:
- Dataset: **1900_cPHAM**
- Table: **NT7**, prefix **AZ3**, "Non-White Population by Race by Sex"
- Universe: `Non-White Persons`
- geogLevels confirmed: nation, state, county
- Variables:
  - `AZ3001` = "Other Colored >> Male"
  - `AZ3002` = "Other Colored >> Female"
  - `AZ3003` = "Negro >> Male"  ← Black
  - `AZ3004` = "Negro >> Female"  ← Black
  - (Black total = AZ3003 + AZ3004)

White:
- **1900_cPHAM NT64**, prefix **AZ2**, "Total White Population", universe `White Persons`,
  `AZ2001` = "Total". (By sex: **NT63 / AZ1**.)

Everyone:
- **1900_cPHAM NT1**, prefix **AYM**, "Total Population", universe `Persons`, `AYM001` = "Total".

Trap: 1900 names Black people "Negro" and puts everyone else non-white in "Other Colored", so unlike
1890 the Black figure is unambiguous here. "White" (AZ2001) includes people who would later be
counted as Hispanic.

## 1900 / urban — NOT AVAILABLE by race

- **1900_cPHAM NT2**, prefix **AYT**, "Population by Urban/Rural Residence", universe `Persons`,
  nation + state + county: `AYT001` = "Urban", `AYT002` = "Rural".
- **1900_cPHAM NT3 / AY4** "Population in Cities of 25,000 and Over" — a total.
- Reason: neither is crossed by race. Urban is available for **everyone** at nation and state in
  1900 but not for any group, so the group cells are `not_measured` and only an all-races urban share
  can be shown.

## 1900 / homeownership — NOT AVAILABLE by race

The home-tenure tables exist and are complete, but carry no race:
- **1900_cPHAM NT61**, prefix **AZZ**, "Number of Homes by Type of Home by Tenure Type", universe
  `Homes`, nation + state + county:
  - `AZZ001` "Farm homes >> Owned free", `AZZ002` "Farm homes >> Owned with encumbrance",
    `AZZ003` "Farm homes >> Encumbrance status unknown", `AZZ004` "Farm homes >> Hired/rented",
    `AZZ005` "Farm homes >> Tenure status unknown"
  - `AZZ006`-`AZZ010` the same five for "Other/non-farm homes"
- **1900_cPHAM NT60 / AZY** "Total Number of Homes by Type of Home", universe `Homes`:
  `AZY001` "Farm homes", `AZY002` "Other/non-farm homes".
- **1900_cAg NT67 / AYI** "Farm Homes by Tenure", universe `Farm Homes`: `AYI001` "Owned: Owned
  free", `AYI002` "Owned: Owned mortgaged", `AYI003` "Owned: Encumbrance unknown", `AYI004`
  "Rented", `AYI005` "Tenure unknown"; total in **NT66 / AYH** `AYH001`.
- Reason: no race-crossed home-tenure table exists in NHGIS for 1900. An all-races homeownership
  rate can be built (owned / (owned + rented), dropping the unknowns), but the by-race cells are
  `not_measured`. Do not pair AZZ with a race table.

## 1900 / literacy — VERIFIED only for native-born males 21 and over

Self-contained spec (numerator and base in one table):
- Dataset: **1900_cPHAM**
- Table: **NT10**, prefix **AYN**, "Native-Born Males 21 Years of Age and Over by Race by Literacy"
- Universe: `Native Males 21 Years and Over`
- geogLevels confirmed: nation, state, county
- Variables:
  - `AYN001` = "White >> Literate"
  - `AYN002` = "White >> Illiterate"
  - `AYN003` = "Negro >> Literate"  ← Black
  - `AYN004` = "Negro >> Illiterate"  ← Black
  - `AYN005` = "Colored >> Literate"  (all non-white, includes Negro)
  - `AYN006` = "Colored >> Illiterate"  (all non-white, includes Negro)
- Base for each race = that race's Literate + Illiterate. No second table needed.
- Companion (no race): **NT11 / AYO** "Foreign-Born Males 21 Years of Age and Over by Citizenship
  Status by Literacy".

Full-universe illiterate counts exist but have **no denominator**:
- **1900_cPHAM NT16**, prefix **AYS**, "Illiterate Population 10 Years of Age and Over by
  Race/Nativity", universe `Illiterate Persons 10 Years and Over`:
  - `AYS001` = "White: Native-born of native parentage"
  - `AYS002` = "White: Native-born of foreign parentage"
  - `AYS003` = "White: Foreign-born"
  - `AYS004` = "Colored, including Negro"
  - `AYS005` = "Negro"
- The matching base, population 10 and over by race, is **not published**: the only 10-and-over base
  in 1900 is **1900_sOcc NT1 / AZ5** "Total Population 10 Years of Age and Over by Sex", universe
  `Persons 10 Years and Over`, with `AZ5001` = "Male" and `AZ5002` = "Female" — sex only, no race.

Traps: the usable 1900 literacy rate covers **native-born males 21 and over**, a much narrower
universe than the 1880 10-and-over rate; `AYS004` "Colored, including Negro" and `AYS005` "Negro"
overlap, so never add them; and `AYN005`/`AYN006` "Colored" likewise contains `AYN003`/`AYN004`
"Negro".

## 1900 / school_attendance — NOT AVAILABLE

- The **bases** exist: **1900_cPHAM NT12 / AYP** "White Population 5 to 20 Years of Age by Nativity
  by Sex" (universe `White Persons 5 to 20 Years of Age`: `AYP001`-`AYP004`) and **NT13 / AYQ**
  "Non-White Population 5 to 20 Years of Age by Race by Sex" (universe `Non-White Persons 5 to 20
  Years of Age`: `AYQ001` "Negro >> Male", `AYQ002` "Negro >> Female", `AYQ003` "Other Colored >>
  Male", `AYQ004` "Other Colored >> Female").
- **Reason it is a gap: there is no attendance or enrollment numerator.** A search of every table
  description in 1900_cPHAM, 1900_cAg and 1900_sOcc for "school", "attend" and "pupil" returns
  nothing. 1900 has school-age population by race but nothing to put over it.

## 1900 / farm_tenancy — VERIFIED

- Dataset: **1900_cPHAM**, "Population, Housing, Agriculture & Manufacturing Data [US, States & Counties]"
- Table: **NT51**, prefix **AZO**, "Number of Farms by Race of Farmer by Tenure of Farmer"
- Universe: `Farms`
- geogLevels confirmed: nation, state, county
- breakdownValues: none required
- Variables:
  - `AZO001` = "White >> Owner"
  - `AZO002` = "White >> Part owner"
  - `AZO003` = "White >> Owner and tenant"
  - `AZO004` = "White >> Farm manager"
  - `AZO005` = "White >> Cash tenant"
  - `AZO006` = "White >> Share tenant"
  - `AZO007` = "Colored >> Owner"
  - `AZO008` = "Colored >> Part owner"
  - `AZO009` = "Colored >> Owner and tenant"
  - `AZO010` = "Colored >> Farm manager"
  - `AZO011` = "Colored >> Cash tenant"
  - `AZO012` = "Colored >> Share tenant"
- Denominator: sum of that race's six cells, cross-checked against **1900_cPHAM NT50**, prefix
  **AZN**, "Total Number of Farms by Race of Farmer", universe `Farms`: `AZN001` = "White",
  `AZN002` = "Colored".
- Tenant/sharecropper share = (Cash tenant + Share tenant) / all six of that race.
- Identical table in the agriculture dataset if it is preferred: **1900_cAg NT7**, prefix **AYJ**,
  "Race of Farmer by Detailed Management", universe `Farms`, nation + state + county — `AYJ001`-
  `AYJ006` "White >> Farm owners / Part owners of farms / Owners and tenants of farms / Farm
  managers / Cash tenants / Share tenants", `AYJ007`-`AYJ012` the same six for "Colored"; base
  **1900_cAg NT6 / AYA** "Race of Farmer" (`AYA001` "White", `AYA002` "Colored"). Prefer the
  1900_cPHAM pair so population and tenancy come from one dataset in one extract.
- Traps: **"Colored" here is the 1900 non-white aggregate, not "Negro"** — this is the one 1900
  condition where the Black figure is not isolable, so label the slice accordingly. The unit is
  farms (operators), not people or households. "Part owner" and "Owner and tenant" are mixed
  categories and belong in neither a clean owner nor a clean tenant bucket; the method doc's rule
  (tenants and sharecroppers group with laboring work) covers Cash tenant and Share tenant only.
  Non-race tenure totals for the same year are in **1900_cAg NT5 / AXZ** "Farm Management"
  (`AXZ001` "Owned", `AXZ002` "Cash and share tenant farms", `AXZ003` "Operated by managers").
