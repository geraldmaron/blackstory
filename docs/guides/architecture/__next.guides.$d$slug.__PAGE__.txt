2:T501,<h1 id="architecture"><a class="heading-anchor" href="#architecture">Architecture</a></h1>
<p>BlackStory publishes evidence-backed Black history connected to people and places. Public web
and staff administration share <code>apps/web</code>. Supabase Postgres and Storage hold canonical data
and media; Vercel hosts the web application behind Cloudflare.</p>
<p>The maintained source is <a href="https://github.com/geraldmaron/blackstory/blob/main/docs/architecture.md">Architecture</a>,
with a <a href="https://github.com/geraldmaron/blackstory/blob/main/docs/decisions-carryover.md">current engineering contract</a>
and <a href="https://github.com/geraldmaron/blackstory/blob/main/docs/research/README.md">research framework</a>.
These describe implementation owners, known gaps, and the evidence needed to challenge a choice.
Historical decision documents do not overrule current requirements or observed behavior.</p>
<p>Research commands run explicitly, independently of a chat session. They stage proposals and
cannot publish. The kernel, durable external-worker protocol and private evidence-level retrieval
share explicit contracts. Their operational and quality limits are documented in the research framework.
